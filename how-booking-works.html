Why the Times Look the Way They Do

We book on the light.

Exteriors look best with the front of the house lit and interiors need strong daylight. So shoots finish well before late afternoon, and earlier in the winter months. No Sundays.

Before We Arrive

Ready in ten minutes.

  • Turn on every light, including lamps and under-cabinet
  • Open blinds and curtains
  • Clear counters, sinks and the fridge door
  • Move cars, bins and hoses out of the front
  • Toilet lids down, towels folded or removed
  • Pets out for the shoot, if possible
  • Vacant unit? Make sure the power is on
  • Share the access details when you book
